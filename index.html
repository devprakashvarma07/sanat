Questions?

The things worth knowing before you let Sanat in.

Is Sanat really free?

Yes. The app is free with no subscription. You use your own AI provider key, and everyday use fits inside free tiers.

Is Sanat just a chatbot wrapper?

No. The AI supplies the words. The agent loop, terminal tools, permission checks, build/test/verify cycle and checkpoints are Sanat's own.

What does Sanat actually do?

Writes and edits code, runs project commands, builds and tests, previews apps, reads screenshots of errors, and manages Git — by voice, on your phone.

What do I need to start?

An Android phone, one free API key, and one project folder you authorize. That's it.

Is my key safe?

Encrypted on-device, never in generated code, never in logs or chat.

Can I build an app by voice on Android?

Yes. Describe the app to Sanat, and it handles coding, building, preview and fixes. No laptop or typing needed.

Which free AI models work with Sanat?

Sanat connects through OpenRouter and other providers, so you can use free tiers of Gemini, DeepSeek, Qwen, Llama and more with your own key.
