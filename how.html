How Sanat works.

One key. One folder. Then talk. The free voice AI coding agent for Android, explained.

Set up in 3 steps.

01 · MAKE

Get your key.

Free to make in OpenRouter or Google AI Studio. Free to use inside free tiers. Your own key, your control.

02 · PASTE

Add it once.

Paste it in Sanat settings. Stored encrypted with Android Keystore. Never leaves your phone.

03 · TALK

Start talking.

Authorize one project folder, then say Hey Sanat. Your AI bill stays ₹0 in free tiers.

What happens next?

Sanat plans, codes, builds, tests and fixes — then reports back. Read the FAQ or see why Sanat is free.

A soul for your Android.

Sanat is a free voice AI coding agent for Android. Say Hey Sanat in Hindi, English or Hinglish and it writes code, runs terminal commands, builds and tests your project, previews the result and fixes errors — on your phone, with your own AI key.
